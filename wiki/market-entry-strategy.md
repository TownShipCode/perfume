# Market Entry Strategy — Pre-Production Questions

**Created:** 2026-08-08
**Status:** WE HAVE NOT YET GONE INTO PRODUCTION. Answer these before committing any capital. This is the market-entry due-diligence list.

---

## 1. MANUFACTURING / SUPPLY (most critical — the whole model depends on it)

- Does **the contract manufacturer HOLD INVENTORY**, or produce on demand? *(Our entire zero-capital model assumes they hold stock and dropship.)*
- If they won't hold stock: can we get **consignment terms** (they carry it, we sell it)?
- **Cost of manufacturing per unit** by size (40ml / 80ml / 100ml)?
- **MOQ** (minimum order quantity) per SKU and per order?
- Lead time: order → dispatch?
- Their cost price points vs our planned wholesale (R40–R100) — what's our margin per bottle?
- **Backup manufacturers:** SensoryFX (Centurion), Sensetek (Sandton) — pricing, lead times, minimums?
- Bottle/packaging cost per unit if we go branded?
- Who pays courier — us, manufacturer, or agent?

## 2. BRAND / PRODUCT RANGE (own brand first — CORRECTED 2026-08-08)

- **Own brand from day 1:** can the contract manufacturer **private-label under our Zen brand** (our name on the bottle/box) AND hold inventory?
- What is the **own-label launch cost** (bottle, cap, label, box, MOQ) per unit by size?
- Can the manufacturer blend to our scent spec (or use their existing dupes under our brand)?
- **Dual-source**: can BOTH our primary contract manufacturer AND SensoryFX/Sensetek private-label for us (mandatory hedge)?
- **Licensing (LATER, optional):** at what scale do we revisit licensing Motala/P2D/Parfumo to broaden the shelf?
- Which **20 bestseller SKUs to seed FIRST** under the Zen brand? (launch lean, not 99)
- Gift sets + multiple sizes (15ml–200ml) — which to add first?
- Target retail price per tier (R30 / R80 / R150 / R200)?

## 3. CUSTOMER / AGENT DEMAND

- Who are the first 20–50 seed agents and where? (townships, campuses, salons, taxi ranks)
- What does an agent actually sell 50 bottles/month of? (validate bestseller list)
- What retail price do customers actually pay in townships (R80? R150? R200?)?
- Is there demand for **branded** (Motala-style) vs generic dupes? Which sells faster?
- What's the agent churn rate in competitor programs? Why do agents quit?

## 4. LOGISTICS / DISTRIBUTION

- Is Courier Guy R65 flat viable at scale? Pargo/Pudo pickup alternatives?
- Who pays return shipping on wrong/defective orders?
- How do we handle **cash-based hawkers** (no card)?
- Does the Mthatha / Eastern Cape market need local fulfillment, or does courier suffice?

## 5. LEGAL / COMPLIANCE

- **VAT registration** — threshold and timing?
- **Consumer Protection Act (CPA)** — returns policy obligations?
- Is "inspired by / dupe" marketing legally safe in SA? Trademark implications?
- **MLM/pyramid compliance** — our 5% commission on orders only (not recruitment) must be documented as affiliate marketing, not a pyramid.
- **IFRA compliance** of oils? (SensoryFX/Sensetek are IFRA-certified — only source compliant.)

## 6. MARKETING / CHANNELS

- Which acquisition channel converts first: WhatsApp groups, FB ads, TikTok, campus reps?
- CAC target (< R100 / active agent)?
- Do we need paid ads in month 1, or does organic + WhatsApp groups suffice at zero capital?

## 7. PAYMENTS / CASHFLOW

- Yoco integration on WhatsApp — when?
- Payment terms for agents (prepay? COD?) — cashflow protection?
- What's our working capital requirement if the manufacturer needs prepayment?

Answer these → produce a one-page market entry strategy: launch SKUs, price ladder, first 50 agents, fulfillment path, launch capital needed (if any), 90-day targets.
