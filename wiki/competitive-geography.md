# Competitive Geography — FFC vs M-Scents Physical Footprint

**Created:** 2026-08-08
**Purpose:** Contrast the physical footprints of Fine Fragrance Collection and M-Scents to reveal how each competes, where they overlap, and where Zen's open market is.

---

## The Two Footprints

### Fine Fragrance Collection — NATIONAL (15 warehouses + mall kiosks)

East London, Port Elizabeth (EC) · Bloemfontein (FS) · Randburg, Vereeniging, Eastgate, Pretoria, Johannesburg (GP) · Durban, Pietermaritzburg (KZN) · Polokwane (Limpopo) · Nelspruit, Witbank (MP) · Rustenburg (NW) · Cape Town (WC)

Plus: physical kiosks in shopping centres + "Find an Agent" suburb-search web tool.

**Positioning:** *National scale dupe brand.* Warehouse-per-major-city, web + call centre, agents do the in-community selling.

### M-Scents — HYPER-LOCAL (21 stores, ALL KZN)

- Durban CBD (8): Commercial City, Grey Street, London Arcade, Queen Street, Smith Street, West Street, West Walk, Workshop
- Durban area (2): Showroom (Overport), Shallcross (Ridge Mall)
- Pietermaritzburg (3): Edendale (Greater Edendale Mall), Laager Centre, Treasury House
- KZN towns (9): Empangeni, Jozini, Kokstad, Ladysmith, Newcastle, Richards Bay, Stanger (KwaDukuza), Tugela Ferry Mall, Umzimkhulu

**Positioning:** *Local KZN institution.* Retail-first, walk-in shops, deep community penetration.

**Critical detail:** many M-Scents stores sit in **township/peri-urban malls** (Greater Edendale Mall, Tugela Ferry, Umzimkhulu, Jozini, Kokstad) — they plant physical retail *inside township communities.*

## What This Means for Strategy (KEY NOTE — revisit when discussing GTM)

1. **KZN is a two-horse fight.** M-Scents clearly wins retail in KZN (14 stores vs FFC's 2 warehouses). FFC's "national" footprint gets beaten locally wherever a regional player digs in. This is the weakness of warehouse-only national expansion: **it loses in depth.**

2. **Everywhere else is a monopoly by absence.** FFC alone in 7 provinces. The market's mental model is "FFC is the dupe brand" because only they have a national sign on the door.

3. **Zen's geographic opening (do NOT enter KZN first):**
   - **Gauteng townships** (Soweto, Tembisa, Alexandra, Katlehong, Mamelodi) — FFC = city warehouses only, M-Scents absent entirely. **#1 launch geography.**
   - **Eastern Cape** — both competitors thin; M-Scents' reported Mthatha opening (first EC store) signals real demand. **#2 focus region.**
   - **KZN = saturated.** Let FFC and M-Scents bleed there.

4. **The third model:** neither competes on people-distribution. FFC competes on reach (national warehouses), M-Scents on presence (local stores). Zen's answer: **"distribution via people, not properties"** — 500 agents = 500 township stores with zero rent. Hyper-local AND national at once.
