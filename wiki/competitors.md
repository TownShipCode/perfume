## Research Methodology

For each competitor, we document:
- Website, social media, value proposition
- Product range, pricing model, SKU count

## Gaps We Can Exploit

1. **No WhatsApp** — Fine Fragrance is web-only. This is our biggest differentiator.
2. **No automated commission** — Their agent model appears manual.
3. **Slow support** — Call centre vs instant WhatsApp replies.
4. **No team hierarchy** — No team member → agent structure visible.
5. **No manufacturer dashboard** — We can give manufacturers direct fulfillment access.
6. **No stock command** — Our "stock Rose Oud" WhatsApp command has no equivalent.
